# AtomsAI brand

Make AtomsAI work feel senior, practical, precise and accountable for what it ships. Preserve the foundations below while adapting the layout to the requested medium.

## Start here

1. Identify the deliverable, audience, dimensions and output format from the brief. Use the user's chosen application and available tools; this skill does not require a particular agent, framework or design app.
2. Start with Deep green and Cream, Founders Grotesk headlines, PP Neue Montreal body text, generous space and a clear hierarchy. Use the supplied master logo.
3. Read the relevant sections below. Fetch only the assets needed for this task. Review the linked previews when composition or artwork is involved.
4. Check the actual exported result at its intended size against the final checklist. Report any substitutions or unresolved limitations.

Source: [brand guidelines](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/). This file is a self-contained snapshot; the current written guidelines and master artwork take precedence if they change. Application mockups illustrate possibilities and do not override explicit rules. If a source or asset cannot be accessed, use this snapshot and disclose the missing asset; do not invent a replacement logo or claim to have inspected an unavailable reference.

## Identity and language

- Write **AtomsAI**, one word, capital A and capital AI. Use the same weight as surrounding text. Do not write “Atoms AI”, “AtomsAi”, “Atoms.ai” or “ATOMSAI”. Lowercase `atomsai` is reserved for web addresses and handles.
- The brand's stated idea is “We don’t sell AI. We ship your workflow.” Its communication is concrete: engineers embed with the client, work in existing systems, ship workflows and measure outcomes.
- Write direct, specific copy about the work and the result. Favor clear verbs and short, useful statements over AI hype. This voice guidance follows the guide's examples; do not invent customer results, capabilities or promises.
- Existing headline examples include “The future isn’t imagined. It’s built.” and “AtomsAI ships AI that works. In production.” Adapt the message to the brief instead of repeating a slogan everywhere.

## Logo and app icon

Always place the supplied SVG artwork. The mark consists of two mirrored sets of three circular forms cut into vertical blades. Do not approximate it with text, generic waveforms or newly drawn geometry.

| Property | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Master mark | 440 × 400 viewBox; preserve its 1.1:1 aspect ratio. |
| Master logotype | 1132 × 200 viewBox. Mark and custom-kerned wordmark are one fixed lockup. |
| Wordmark | “Atoms” regular and “AI” bold in the artwork. Never retype it or apply this weight split to the name in normal copy. |
| Proportions | In the lockup, mark = 220 × 200; wordmark starts at x=280. Gap = 60, or 0.3 × mark height. Capitals span y=20–180, or 80% of mark height. |
| Mark clear space | Half the mark's width on each side; half its height above and below. |
| Logotype clear space | One mark width on each side; one mark height above and below the entire lockup. |
| Placement | Mark: top, middle or bottom, at the left or right margin. Logotype: top, middle or bottom, always at the left margin. Preserve asymmetrical negative space. |
| Minimum logotype | 100 px wide digitally; 25 mm wide in print (approximately 1 in). Below this, use the standalone mark. |
| Preferred pairing | Cream artwork on Deep green, or Deep green artwork on Cream. The library also supplies Black and White variants for monochrome use. Never invent other logo colorways. |
| Integrity | Do not stretch, rotate, crop, redraw, add effects, change kerning, or rescale/rearrange the mark and wordmark separately. |

The app icon is the Cream mark centered on Deep green, spanning **60% of container width**. Use the supplied circle or rounded square; the latter has radius 40 in a 230-unit container. App icons, favicons and social avatars use this asset when the full logotype cannot fit. The app icon does not replace the logotype in layouts or serve as a sign-off. Do not confuse this container rule with the standalone mark's clear space.

Use Iris or Orange for focal elements, Iris Dark or Crimson for depth, and Iris Light or Apricot for light surfaces. Choose one accent family per layout, with focal accents near 10% of Forest-led compositions. Use Black labels on Iris and Orange CTAs, Iris Dark text on Iris Light, and Crimson text on Apricot. Forest shade uses 5535 C / U as a dark green reference candidate. All spot references require physical proof; none is a press-approved match.

### Accent stacking

Stack dark, mid and light tones within one family: Iris Dark → Iris → Iris Light or Crimson → Orange → Apricot. Use opaque fills and clean edges. Keep the lightest layer on top.

### Ramps and combinations

- Forest ramp, dark to light: Forest shade → Deep green → Forest green → Sage → Sage tint → Stone → Cream.
- Neutral ramp, dark to light: Forest shade → Charcoal → Graphite → Slate → Ash → Mist → Paper.
- True-grey ramp for UI and ordered data, dark to light: Black → Grey 5 → Grey 4 → Grey 3 → Grey 2 → Grey 1 → White.
- Use ramps to express hierarchy, states and data. Do not imply that every neighboring pair is suitable for readable text.

Approved combinations use roughly **60% lead / 30% support / 10% accent** by visual area, not a rigid pixel quota:

## Glass styling

Build glass with the supplied CSS reference's static layers: White at 10% → 30% → 10% in a −75° gradient, backdrop blur, fixed −45° screen reflection, inset lighting, conic rim, contact shadow and separate blurred shadow ring. Match the corners to the component’s shape; set `--glass-radius` to any CSS border-radius value. The 0.6em fallback and pill class are examples, not restrictions. No animation, transitions, hover scaling or active-state rotation.

Set font size on `atoms-glass-wrap` to scale the entire component. Use em throughout the material, without pixel clamps: backdrop blur 0.375em; rim 0.0625em; contact shadow 0 / 0.25 / 0.125 / −0.125em in Black at 20%. The detached shadow ring uses Black 20% → 10% and 0.125em blur. Preserve the reference's inset lighting and use a subtle 15% White screen reflection with a broad, eased cosine falloff. Apply shadows only to the glass surface, never to text, icons or other contents. Get the complete wrapper markup and exact values from the downloadable kit. Keep live labels above the reflection; use Cream on checked dark areas and Deep green on light backgrounds. Check the final crop against 4.5:1 for normal text or use the opaque fallback.

For dark mode, add `atoms-glass--dark` to the wrapper: Black fill at 25% → 40% → 25%, Cream text, White rim 8%, reflection 6%, lower highlight 6% and inner glow 2%. It shares the light version's dimensions, eased reflection and black shadows. Use Forest shade for the opaque fallback. Apply the modifier when the host application's dark theme is active.

The native Figma panels informed the compositions; the user-supplied CSS defines this web material. Render the surface as live HTML/CSS, never as a panel screenshot. Light and dark inputs sit on flat Mist and Forest green; the empty cards share the existing pattern renderer for comparison. Add `atoms-glass--solid` to the wrapper for an opaque surface. Unsupported backdrop-filter, reduced transparency, increased contrast and print use opaque fills; forced colors use the system palette.

Preview: [glass styling](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/glass/). Download: [glass kit](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/assets/glass/atomsai-glass.zip) with CSS, JSON, standalone HTML and usage notes.

## Typography

Use this web scale at **768 px and wider** for desktop and **below 768 px** for mobile. Size / line-height values are CSS pixels. Implement sizes in rem (16 px base) with unitless leading so text scales with user preferences.

| Role | Family | Desktop size / line-height | Mobile size / line-height | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Founders Grotesk | 80 / 72 px | 48 / 43.2 px | −1.5% |
| H1 | Founders Grotesk | 64 / 64 px | 40 / 40 px | −1.5% |
| H2 | Founders Grotesk | 48 / 48 px | 32 / 32 px | −1.5% |
| H3 | Founders Grotesk | 32 / 32 px | 24 / 24 px | −1.5% |
| Body | PP Neue Montreal | 16 / 22.4 px | 16 / 22.4 px | Normal |
| Caption | PP Neue Montreal | 14 / 19.6 px | 14 / 19.6 px | Normal |

Regular 400 is the default for every role; Medium 500 is available for emphasis. Display uses 90% leading, H1–H3 use 100%, and body/caption use 140%. Use Display for short hero statements. Keep semantic heading levels independent of visual size; an H1 can use Display styling. Body stays at 16 px on mobile, with roughly 45–75 characters per line. Captions are for supporting details, not running text. Use the separate Google Workspace settings for slides and documents.

- Headlines use **sentence case with punctuation**. Break lines deliberately and avoid a lone final word.
- Tighten leading as display size increases; open it for smaller text. Keep glyphs, accents and descenders from colliding or clipping.
- Use default character forms. Use tabular figures for tables and data, and verify required language glyphs in the output.
- The guideline website itself uses 32 px Founders Grotesk and 14 px PP Neue Montreal for its shell. Do not limit a slide, poster or product to those two sizes.
- Use the actual font files and real weights rather than synthetic bold. WOFF2 files are supplied for web use. Desktop applications may need licensed desktop-format fonts; if unsupported, report the limitation and agree a substitute instead of silently calling a fallback on-brand.

For shared slides and documents, use Chivo and Inter as the approved free alternatives. Replace a 48 pt Founders Grotesk headline with 41 pt Chivo Regular at −4% total tracking or 41.5 pt Chivo Semibold (600) at −2.5%. Check wrapping where tracking controls are unavailable. Match the primary font’s baseline spacing in both columns. For a 48 pt Founders headline at 90% leading, use 43.2 pt leading for both families. For 12 pt PP Neue Montreal Regular at 140% leading, use 11.25 pt Inter Regular at −2% tracking and the same 16.8 pt leading.

## Icons

Use the existing stroke library first. Icons have a `0 0 24 24` viewBox, no fill, one uniform stroke, **miter joins** and **butt caps**. Regular is **1.5 units**; Medium is **2 units**, measured at 24 px. Preserve the deliberate open gaps at crossings and detached arrow shafts. Scale the stroke proportionally with the icon; do not use non-scaling strokes.

Regular pairs with body/interface text. Medium pairs with headlines or Medium labels and can help at small sizes. Inline icons take the label's color and a consistent gap; lead with an action icon, trail with a navigation arrow. Avoid generic rounded icon sets, filled silhouettes, multi-color icons and decorative containers baked into the SVG.

For new icons, use the separate [icon creation skill](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/skills/atomsai-icons/SKILL.md), which includes measured geometry and crossing examples. Preview: [icon style and library](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/icons/).

## Patterns

Patterns are interference fields derived from light through parallel slits, not generic gradients, decorative stripes or repeated logo marks. Prefer an export from the [pattern generator](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/tools/#pattern-generator).

| Structure | Character and use |
| --- | --- |
| Drift | Interleaved light from the top and bottom; calm, balanced backgrounds. |
| Offset | Tapered, offset spikes rising from the base; directional energy. |
| Single band | One luminous band across the middle; strong and symmetrical. |
| Concentric | Even rings radiating from a center; focus on one point or moment. |

- Published slit densities are 5, 10, 20 and 40. Coarse reads at small sizes; fine adds texture to larger surfaces. The generator additionally provides 6, 8, 13, 16, 25 and 32 slit stops.
- Concentric uses only its two existing presets: density indices 1 and 2, shown as 10 and 20 in the generator. Do not fabricate extra ring densities from the general slit scale.
- Published colorways are Forest, Sage, Graphite and Iris. Forest is the default; Iris is occasional. Preserve the generator's full layer recipe, including its internal reflection values.
- Use **one structure per composition**. Crop, bleed, rotate and scale uniformly to suit the format. Never stretch, skew, warp or mix structures.
- Export the required dimensions using the generator. A link to the generator is not an image file. If the tool cannot be operated, request an existing export or use a permitted flat surface; do not claim a generic imitation is official artwork.

Preview: [structures, density and usage](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/patterns/).

## Illustration

Use fine monochrome linework, translucent overlapping layers and an Iris edge fade. The only preview/export pairings are **Black bg** (white outlines on Black, default) and **White bg** (black outlines on White). For new illustrations and changes to the interactive examples, use the [Illustration Skill](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/skills/atomsai-illustrations/SKILL.md).

**Concept drawings:** one idea in simple outlined 2D/3D geometry. Small solid nodes and arrows may emphasize connections. Existing concepts are Full Visibility, Built In Your Stack and Compounding Outcomes.

**Construction:** build complete 3D solids, planes, hoops and curves with the shared orthographic camera. Match the reference at the neutral pose and verify it from other sides. Do not reconstruct only the front-facing SVG paths and assign invented depth. Each gallery preview has its own responsive p5.js canvas; one depth-aware projection supplies both direct canvas drawing and vector export. Generate SVG on copy/download, not during dragging. Reuse static masters for ordinary placement and comparison.

**Linework:** one centered object per card with generous space. Use scene depth to separate hidden and visible segments, with one shared 35% opacity group for all hidden linework and full opacity for visible outlines. Overlapping surfaces must never compound the opacity. Paint color fills first, then hidden and visible linework. Highlighted outlines render last at 100%, including behind other surfaces. Always on and Versatility are opaque exceptions: omit hidden edges and nodes. Versatility keeps quadrant dividers on all six faces, revealed by rotation, and its Iris face is visible only from the outside. Draw intentional edges and silhouettes, not mesh tessellation. Highlight one focal part, preserving approved exceptions such as Stack 2's two end layers.

**Gradient fills:** use Iris #A77BCA from the current brand palette for illustration fills. For isometric rectangular surfaces, use a diamond gradient aligned to the four corners of the face: transparent at the center, 25% opacity halfway out and full Iris at every edge, with a smooth quadratic fade. Do not use an elliptical radial fill on these planes. Curved surfaces retain a radial gradient with cubic opacity: transparent in the center, 12.5% opacity at the midpoint and full color at the edge. Keep the gradient inside the existing silhouette and retain transparent centers. Do not add a pale rim, a separate darker band or an outer glow. The same gradient spans all faces of a highlighted part; internal face seams do not restart it.

Existing masters are normalized to a 1 px outline at 200 px artwork width. Preserve geometry, aspect ratios and viewBoxes, including non-zero origins. Scale artwork and strokes together; retain the shared padded viewport and normalized line weight when extending the p5 previews.

Use one lead illustration per slide, hero or editorial layout. A feature grid may contain up to three, one per card. Apply this limit per section on long pages. Use one illustration mode per group and clear space of at least one eighth of the artwork width.

Minimum digital frame width is 200 px for simple isometric objects and 280 px for concepts or dense detail. Scale the master stroke with the artwork. For the normalized Focus master, rendered stroke = display width / 200. Widths of 200, 280, 420 and 560 px yield strokes of 1.00, 1.40, 2.10 and 2.80 px. At final size, keep lines at least 1 px and visible gaps at least 2 px. Enlarge drawings that fail this check. Use icons below the minimum sizes and proof print work at final physical size.

## AI identity

**Signal** is the functional AI expression of the mark. All four states reuse its original three mirrored pairs of circular blades. Thinking moves three slits on each half continuously outward. Speaking contracts all six slits together through the original openings, with irregular timing and varied strength. Retain the mark's inward-facing circular arcs and taper; never substitute equalizer bars. Use it at the point of interaction; retain the original logo for brand signatures.

| State | Behavior | When to show it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle | Complete, still mark | Ready; no task in progress |
| Listening | 2.4 s inward rhythm, outside → center | Input capture is active |
| Thinking | Three slits per half travel center → outside, one position every 750 ms; close before recycling at the center | A submitted task is pending; never a progress percentage |
| Speaking | All six slits contract together in speech-like phrases: soft syllables, stronger accents, overlapping releases and longer pauses. Keep the original slit openings fixed and all pairs synchronized | Audible output is playing; stop on interruption |

Use Deep green on Cream or the inverse; Black and White for monochrome. Keep the ink consistent across states. Pair the element with an adjacent visible status label, announced politely only on state changes. Hide the decorative SVG from assistive technology. Show a still pose with reduced motion (Thinking uses the complete logo); below 48 px use the complete static mark with the current label. Prefer 64–160 px width and retain half the width/height of clear space on each respective side. Use one active Signal per conversation.

Drive states from actual events. Text interactions may skip Listening and Speaking. Cancellation returns to Idle; errors stop motion and require a visible message and recovery action. Keep microphone, stop and retry controls separate. The downloaded animation is a fallback cadence, not evidence of live audio capture.

Use the supplied animation engine for state changes: `createSignal(svg, signalMotion)` then `signal.setState(...)`. It interpolates the positions and uniform scales of the same six circles, together with slit geometry, over 600 ms. All shapes remain fully opaque; closed slits contribute no spatial weight during recycling. No crossfade or shape swapping. Preserve continuity during rapid interruptions. Update the status label immediately. Pause/offscreen/hidden states freeze the clock; reduced motion uses still poses. Call `destroy()` when unmounting.

## Motion and composition

Use the existing [mark reveal tool](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/tools/#mark-reveal) for animation. It runs a **4 s cycle**, with **0.88 s** for each hide/reveal move and **0.2 s** stagger per pair, outside to center, using `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.3, 1)`. Circles retreat through vertical slits and return; do not morph or distort the static master. Respect reduced motion with a complete static mark. The tool exports MP4 or WebM according to browser support.

Build hierarchy with strong display type, quiet supporting copy, aligned edges and generous negative space. These are applications of the existing examples, not a fixed spacing grid for every medium. Favor one clear message and one dominant visual idea. Keep logo clear space independent of layout margins.

Use the [application gallery](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/examples/) for reference: social profile/posts/stories, office screens/displays, transit posters, vertical banners, event cards and business cards. Adapt to aspect ratio and reading distance. Do not treat names, contact details, event dates or claims inside mockups as production content.

## Photography and people

The photography idea is “In their element”: people with character, a sense of place and something happening in the frame. Use a deliberate portrait, a close encounter, movement or a revealing detail according to the story. Vary distance, setting and light while keeping skin and local color believable. Place typography, logos and patterns beside the photograph. Never tint skin to match the palette or cover faces and key actions with effects. For image generation and editing, use the [Photography Skill](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/skills/atomsai-photography/).

- **Customer stories:** the actual customer and their point of view. Let an environmental portrait, encounter or detail come from the story; a photograph need not demonstrate software use. Pair approved names and quotations with real subjects. Use a text-led story and approved customer logo when photography is unavailable.
- **Support:** a close listening moment for editorial use; a real head-and-shoulders portrait for the assigned person; the brand mark for a shared team; Signal with an explicit “AI assistant” label for automation. Keep names and roles visible. Portraits do not indicate availability.
- **Onboarding:** photography at welcome and human handoff moments. Use product UI, icons and existing geometric illustrations for instructions and progress. No invented human mascot.
- **Careers:** actual people and relationships, including individual portraits, moments between tasks or personal interests supplied by a profile. Show engineering work when it adds to the story. Do not invent employee biographies, perks or company places.

Compose for 4:3 stories, 4:5 portraits, 16:9 heroes and circle-safe 1:1 avatars. Preserve proportions and prepare separate mobile crops. Use a 5% working safety inset for essential detail, not print bleed. Place live copy on a solid panel by default. Keep full-resolution profiled masters; convert web derivatives to sRGB without upscaling. Record image source, permission, credit, approved channels and expiry; keep signed releases private.

The AI-generated reference board shows fictional people for art direction only. It must never represent actual customers, staff, testimonials or live support identities.
