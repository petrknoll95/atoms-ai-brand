# AtomsAI brand

Make AtomsAI work feel senior, practical, precise and accountable for what it ships. Preserve the foundations below while adapting the layout to the requested medium.

## Start here

1. Identify the deliverable, audience, dimensions and output format from the brief. Use the user's chosen application and available tools; this skill does not require a particular agent, framework or design app.
2. Start with Deep green and Cream, Founders Grotesk headlines, PP Neue Montreal body text, generous space and a clear hierarchy. Use the supplied master logo.
3. Read the relevant sections below. Fetch only the assets needed for this task. Review the linked previews when composition or artwork is involved.
4. Check the actual exported result at its intended size against the final checklist. Report any substitutions or unresolved limitations.

Source: [brand guidelines](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/). This file is a self-contained snapshot; the current written guidelines and master artwork take precedence if they change. Application mockups illustrate possibilities and do not override explicit rules. If a source or asset cannot be accessed, use this snapshot and disclose the missing asset; do not invent a replacement logo or claim to have inspected an unavailable reference.

## Identity and language

- Write **AtomsAI**, one word, capital A and capital AI. Use the same weight as surrounding text. Do not write “Atoms AI”, “AtomsAi”, “Atoms.ai” or “ATOMSAI”. Lowercase `atomsai` is reserved for web addresses and handles.
- The brand's stated idea is “We don’t sell AI. We ship your workflow.” Its communication is concrete: engineers embed with the client, work in existing systems, ship workflows and measure outcomes.
- Write direct, specific copy about the work and the result. Favor clear verbs and short, useful statements over AI hype. This voice guidance follows the guide's examples; do not invent customer results, capabilities or promises.
- Existing headline examples include “The future isn’t imagined. It’s built.” and “AtomsAI ships AI that works. In production.” Adapt the message to the brief instead of repeating a slogan everywhere.

## Logo and app icon

Always place the supplied SVG artwork. The mark consists of two mirrored sets of three circular forms cut into vertical blades. Do not approximate it with text, generic waveforms or newly drawn geometry.

| Property | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Master mark | 440 × 400 viewBox; preserve its 1.1:1 aspect ratio. |
| Master logotype | 1132 × 200 viewBox. Mark and custom-kerned wordmark are one fixed lockup. |
| Wordmark | “Atoms” regular and “AI” bold in the artwork. Never retype it or apply this weight split to the name in normal copy. |
| Proportions | In the lockup, mark = 220 × 200; wordmark starts at x=280. Gap = 60, or 0.3 × mark height. Capitals span y=20–180, or 80% of mark height. |
| Mark clear space | Half the mark's width on each side; half its height above and below. |
| Logotype clear space | One mark width on each side; one mark height above and below the entire lockup. |
| Placement | Mark: top, middle or bottom, at the left or right margin. Logotype: top, middle or bottom, always at the left margin. Preserve asymmetrical negative space. |
| Minimum logotype | 100 px wide digitally; 25 mm wide in print (approximately 1 in). Below this, use the standalone mark. |
| Preferred pairing | Cream artwork on Deep green, or Deep green artwork on Cream. The library also supplies Black and White variants for monochrome use. Never invent other logo colorways. |
| Integrity | Do not stretch, rotate, crop, redraw, add effects, change kerning, or rescale/rearrange the mark and wordmark separately. |

The app icon is the Cream mark centered on Deep green, spanning **60% of container width**. Use the supplied circle or rounded square; the latter has radius 40 in a 230-unit container. App icons, favicons and social avatars use this asset when the full logotype cannot fit. The app icon does not replace the logotype in layouts or serve as a sign-off. Do not confuse this container rule with the standalone mark's clear space.

Preview: [logo rules and diagrams](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/logo/).

Deep green and Cream are primary and should carry most surfaces, type and layouts. Forest green, Sage tint and Stone support them. Iris is the single accent family; use it sparingly, alongside Forest. Neutral colors support documents, interfaces and dense information.

### Ramps and combinations

- Forest ramp, dark to light: Forest shade → Deep green → Forest green → Sage → Sage tint → Stone → Cream.
- Neutral ramp, dark to light: Forest shade → Charcoal → Graphite → Slate → Ash → Mist → Paper.
- Use ramps to express hierarchy, states and data. Do not imply that every neighboring pair is suitable for readable text.

Approved combinations use roughly **60% lead / 30% support / 10% accent** by visual area, not a rigid pixel quota:

| Lead | Support | Accent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep green | Forest green | Cream |
| Forest green | Paper | Graphite |
| Stone | Forest shade | Sage |
| Cream | Sage tint | Forest green |
| Iris shade | Iris | Iris tint |
| Deep green | Cream | Iris |

The Iris combination is an occasional accent composition, not a replacement for the default Forest identity.

Use Forest by default; Neutral for quieter document and data layouts; Iris sparingly. Check contrast for the actual text, size and surface rather than assuming a named theme passes every use. Target at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and essential interface graphics. Text over patterns needs checking against the local background throughout the text area.

### Gradients

Use soft vertical blends as quiet backgrounds or pattern bases. The eight published recipes are Forest shade → Forest green, Cream → Stone, Forest shade → Iris, Iris tint → Iris shade, Forest shade → Cream, Forest green → Stone, Iris → Iris tint, and Sage tint → Deep green.

## Typography

| Role | Family | Weight | Line-height and spacing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headlines and display | Founders Grotesk | Regular 400; Medium 500 for emphasis | 90–100% leading; −1.5% tracking, or `letter-spacing: -0.015em`. |
| Lead text | Founders Grotesk in the guide's specimen | Regular 400 | General lead guidance: 130%; the 32 px specimen uses 120%. |
| Body, captions and interface copy | PP Neue Montreal | Regular 400; Medium 500 available for emphasis | Body leading 140%; do not automatically apply headline tracking. |

- Headlines use **sentence case with punctuation**. Break lines deliberately and avoid a lone final word.
- Tighten leading as display size increases; open it for smaller text. Keep glyphs, accents and descenders from colliding or clipping.
- The published specimens are **80 px / 90%** display, **32 px / 120%** lead/display, and **16 px / 140%** body. These demonstrate hierarchy; they are not a universal size scale for every format.
- Use default character forms. Use tabular figures for tables and data, and verify required language glyphs in the output.
- The guideline website itself uses 32 px Founders Grotesk and 14 px PP Neue Montreal for its shell. Do not limit a slide, poster or product to those two sizes.
- Use the actual font files and real weights rather than synthetic bold. WOFF2 files are supplied for web use. Desktop applications may need licensed desktop-format fonts; if unsupported, report the limitation and agree a substitute instead of silently calling a fallback on-brand.

Preview: [editable type specimens](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/typography/). [Font library](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/assets/#fonts): the site's notice limits these files to AtomsAI work.

## Icons

Use the existing stroke library first. Icons have a `0 0 24 24` viewBox, no fill, one uniform stroke, **miter joins** and **butt caps**. Regular is **1.5 units**; Medium is **2 units**, measured at 24 px. Preserve the deliberate open gaps at crossings and detached arrow shafts. Scale the stroke proportionally with the icon; do not use non-scaling strokes.

Regular pairs with body/interface text. Medium pairs with headlines or Medium labels and can help at small sizes. Inline icons take the label's color and a consistent gap; lead with an action icon, trail with a navigation arrow. Avoid generic rounded icon sets, filled silhouettes, multi-color icons and decorative containers baked into the SVG.

For new icons, use the separate [icon creation skill](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/skills/atomsai-icons/SKILL.md), which includes measured geometry and crossing examples. Preview: [icon style and library](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/icons/).

## Patterns

Patterns are interference fields derived from light through parallel slits, not generic gradients, decorative stripes or repeated logo marks. Prefer an export from the [pattern generator](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/tools/#pattern-generator).

| Structure | Character and use |
| --- | --- |
| Drift | Interleaved light from the top and bottom; calm, balanced backgrounds. |
| Offset | Tapered, offset spikes rising from the base; directional energy, quiet area above for type. |
| Single band | One luminous band across the middle; place a headline above or below. |
| Concentric | Even rings radiating from a center; focus on one point or moment. |

- Published slit densities are 5, 10, 20 and 40. Coarse reads at small sizes; fine adds texture to larger surfaces. The generator additionally provides 6, 8, 13, 16, 25 and 32 slit stops.
- Concentric uses only its two existing presets: density indices 1 and 2, shown as 10 and 20 in the generator. Do not fabricate extra ring densities from the general slit scale.
- Published colorways are Forest, Sage, Graphite and Iris. Forest is the default; Iris is occasional. Preserve the generator's full layer recipe, including its internal reflection values.
- Use **one structure per composition**. Crop, bleed, rotate and scale uniformly to suit the format. Never stretch, skew, warp or mix structures.
- Place copy in calm areas, away from bright beams and spikes. If contrast fails, change the crop or give text its own clear surface.
- Export the required dimensions using the generator. A link to the generator is not an image file. If the tool cannot be operated, request an existing export or use a permitted flat surface; do not claim a generic imitation is official artwork.

Preview: [structures, density and usage](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/patterns/).

## Illustration

Two related modes have different fill behavior:

**Concept drawings:** one idea in simple outlined 2D/3D geometry, one thin line weight, one palette ink on a light surface. Small solid nodes and arrows may emphasize connections. No filled planes, shading, gradients or literal imagery. Existing concepts are Full Visibility (map workflows and handoffs), Built In Your Stack (agents inside existing systems), and Compounding Outcomes (each workflow supports the next).

**Isometric linework:** one centered object per card with generous space. Nearer forms hide lines behind them using opaque fills that match the surface. Use Deep green lines by default. Published pairings are Deep green/White, Deep green/Cream, Cream/Deep green and Black/White (line/surface). Keep these fills; making everything transparent exposes hidden edges. Objects are Scale, Focus, Modular, Always on, Insight and Momentum.

The illustration masters use 1.5-unit lines in their own approximately 250–280-unit viewBoxes. This is not the icon system's 1.5 units on a 24-unit grid. Preserve each artwork's aspect ratio, line proportions and viewBox, including non-zero origins.

Preview and color exports: [illustration](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/illustration/).

## Motion and composition

Use the existing [mark reveal tool](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/tools/#mark-reveal) for animation. It runs a **4 s cycle**, with **0.88 s** for each hide/reveal move and **0.2 s** stagger per pair, outside to center, using `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.3, 1)`. Circles retreat through vertical slits and return; do not morph or distort the static master. Respect reduced motion with a complete static mark. The tool exports MP4 or WebM according to browser support.

Build hierarchy with strong display type, quiet supporting copy, aligned edges and generous negative space. These are applications of the existing examples, not a fixed spacing grid for every medium. Favor one clear message and one dominant visual idea. Keep logo clear space independent of layout margins.

Use the [application gallery](https://petrknoll95.github.io/atoms-ai-brand/examples/) for reference: social profile/posts/stories, office screens/displays, transit posters, vertical banners, event cards and business cards. Adapt to aspect ratio and reading distance. Do not treat names, contact details, event dates or claims inside mockups as production content.

## Final check

- Correct spelling and voice; no invented claims or leftover mockup content.
- Master logo, correct colors, aspect ratio, alignment, clear space and minimum size.
- Exact palette, restrained Iris, readable foreground/background pairs and pattern placement.
- Correct families and real weights; sentence-case headlines, tracking and leading; no clipped or overflowing text.
- Consistent icon stroke, sharp joins, flush caps, open gaps and label alignment.
- One pattern structure; no distortion; correct illustration fill behavior.
- Export inspected at final dimensions in the target application. Note any missing licensed font, unavailable asset or agreed exception.
