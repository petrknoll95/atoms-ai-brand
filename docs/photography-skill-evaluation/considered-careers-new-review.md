## Why the first render was not selected

The first render met exact 4:3 dimensions, depicted a high-quality home workspace and had readable natural daylight. It nevertheless placed the engineer's hand at her chin, despite the prompt excluding dramatic chin-resting. That familiar “thinking engineer” pose weakened the desired observed working moment. Its large monitor also prominently displayed invented workflow blocks and a graph, making pseudo-interface content too central.

A single fresh corrective generation was made. The selected replacement improves the work action, removes the chin-resting pose, changes the camera to a three-quarter rear view, gives the person a convincingly functional task chair, and makes the screen content less diagram-like. It does **not** solve wardrobe/casting repetition or fully establish the evaluation/reliability task. No further automatic generation was made.
