Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Create one 4:3 landscape editorial photograph for a careers profile of a fictional research engineer whose morning city bicycle ride gives her time to think away from the screen.

Catch her actually cycling at an unhurried pace along a quiet urban street beside a dark red brick railway viaduct, with pale stone apartment facades visible beyond its arch. She is in her early forties, with an individual, slightly asymmetrical face, short dark curls under a plain cycling helmet, a cobalt blue overshirt and ordinary trousers, riding an unbranded practical city bicycle. Her face is absorbed but alert to the route, unaware of the camera.

Use movement as the main photographic device: a street-level three-quarter side view, a restrained panning effect in the surroundings and turning wheels, with her face and torso crisp. Keep the entire rider and bicycle within the frame and enough architecture to establish a lived-in city. Cool clear morning light and a low lateral sun produce directional shadows; retain readable face detail, true skin color and the distinct blue, brick and stone colors. Natural skin and fabric texture, believable riding balance and bicycle mechanics. No words, company marks, logos, screens, invented film damage or uniform amber wash.
