Retain cardboard fibers, creased fabric and worn concrete, with enough depth of field to read the handoff and the loading area. No camera-facing poses, staged handshake, visible logos, readable labels, embedded headline, watermark or heavy cinematic color grading.

## Assessment

**Both assignments were preserved at the prompt level.** Test 1 retains two workers, an actual parcel transfer, a loading bay, nighttime, 16:9 framing and precisely the RIGHT third for copy. Test 2 provides three distinct, standalone prompts: wide work, close detail and one individual portrait, all in a small bicycle workshop with explicit exclusion of office imagery. Neither request was replaced with a generic office scene, a contact sheet or image generation.

Rules that materially changed the outputs:

- **Explicit brief over defaults:** the night scene uses dock lighting and dark exterior context, not the skill’s usual window light. The reserved copy area is the right third, not the website’s left-side example.
- **Concrete interaction and function:** the parcel is supported by both workers; the bicycle scenes specify tools, contact, a repair stand and a credible repair task. These are operational details, not just mood adjectives.
- **Photographic copy space:** the headline area is a low-detail wall within the photograph, with subjects and gestures safely to the left. It is not a colored panel or rendered headline.
- **Series coherence without repeated framing:** the three workshop prompts repeat light direction, white balance, finish and a small set of material cues while changing distance, aspect ratio and subject treatment. Working subjects attend to their task; the portrait subject looks at the camera.
- **Art direction without forced palette:** cream plaster and subdued materials are compatible with the palette, while the blue bicycle and burgundy tool chest retain their own colors. No green wash or uniform brand-colored wardrobe is imposed.
- **Requested delivery mode:** complete prompts were returned without generation, and no unsupported claim of pixel inspection or exact identity continuity was made.

Ambiguity and limits: the careers request does not specify aspect ratios, exact recurring identities or a required camera position. I chose 16:9, 4:3 and 4:5 to suit the three uses and treated coherence as shared photographic treatment and setting, not an identity match. The portrait can depict a different fictional workshop colleague. Repeated location descriptions help consistency but do not guarantee the same physical room or bicycle across actual generations. The skill’s reference-and-inspection guidance would become relevant if exact continuity were required. No material instruction failure was identified in these prompt-only tests; anatomy, nighttime rendering, empty copy space and generated continuity remain untested.
