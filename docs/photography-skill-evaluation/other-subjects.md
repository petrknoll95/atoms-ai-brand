# Photography skill evaluation: other subjects

Source read: `/Users/petrknoll/Documents/GitHub/atoms-ai-brand/skills/atomsai-photography/SKILL.md` only. Both assignments were executed as independent prompt-writing requests. No image generation, network access, other test results, or repository edits were used.

## Deliverable 1 — red kettle still life

Create a photographic product still life in a square 1:1 format: a vivid red enameled kettle on a brushed-steel surface, photographed from directly overhead with the camera looking straight down at 90 degrees. Show the entire kettle, including its handle and spout, comfortably within the frame. Make the kettle the clear focal point, with the direction of the steel's fine brushed grain providing quiet structure around it. Use a large diffused light source from the upper left and gentle fill, producing soft, believable contact shadows and broad, controlled reflections in the enamel and steel. Preserve the kettle's vivid red color accurately: retain its saturation and hue, with natural variations only where light and reflections fall. Keep the steel neutral, with visible fine grain and realistic metallic reflectivity. Render crisp material detail and functional, physically plausible kettle geometry, with sufficient depth of field to keep the whole product clear. Moderate contrast, no heavy color grading, and no colored cast that shifts the red. No people, plants, text, logos or watermarks.

## Deliverable 2 — coastal footpath at blue hour

Create a vertical 9:16 landscape photograph of a rain-soaked coastal footpath at blue hour, after sunset. View the scene from a natural standing height, looking along the narrow path as it curves from the lower foreground around a rugged headland, with the open sea visible beside it. Let the winding path carry the eye through the full frame; use the whole composition for the landscape, with no area deliberately reserved for copy. Show rain-darkened earth and stone, shallow puddles reflecting the cool sky, wet rock textures and restrained coastal vegetation. Light the scene entirely with believable, dim blue-hour skylight beneath a soft overcast sky. Preserve the naturally cool atmosphere and subtle distinctions between the sea, sky and land; keep shadow detail readable without making the scene look like daylight. Use controlled contrast and natural color, without orange-and-teal grading or exaggerated saturation. Keep the near path and the coastline sufficiently clear to describe their texture and shape, allowing distant coastal haze to soften the horizon naturally. Quiet, observed and photographically credible. No people, buildings, text, logos or watermarks.

## Assessment

- **Non-office, non-people suitability:** Strong. The skill explicitly separates photographic treatment from subject matter and expressly supports products, environments, overhead views, vivid subject colors and night settings. Neither request required adding a person, an office or brand-colored props.
- **Still life:** The prompt preserves the square format, straight overhead view, brushed steel, vivid red and all requested exclusions. The material and lighting guidance gives useful direction for enamel highlights and steel reflections. Explicit precedence for requested product color prevents the default restrained palette from desaturating or recoloring the kettle.
- **Landscape:** The prompt preserves 9:16, blue hour, rain-soaked coastal setting, absence of people and buildings, and no reserved copy space. The outdoor/night exception translates soft light into cool available skylight rather than importing a warm studio or daytime look.
- **Failures observed:** No blocking failures in prompt construction. The skill supplied enough written direction for both assignments without opening optional visual references or asking unnecessary questions.
- **Ambiguities and limits:** “Our kettle” provides no product reference, exact shape or color specification. The prompt can request faithful vivid red rendering, but cannot establish an exact commercial product match or colorimetric accuracy from the brief alone. The skill resolves the possible tension between gently warm whites and blue-hour color through its explicit instruction to preserve the requested scene. No generated images were inspected, so this assessment covers prompt usability and instruction adherence, not visual output quality.
